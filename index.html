Our Story & Craft

A Boutique Patisserie Rooted in Mombasa

RaRa In The Kitchen is a luxury bakery and patisserie located in Mombasa, renowned for creating showstopping customizable cakes, rich chocolate fudge creations, and delicate French pastries. Run by a passionate professional pâtissière, every bake combines the finest local and imported ingredients with uncompromised artistic detail.

Alongside our daily patisserie offerings, we operate RARA École de Pâtisserie in Nyali, nurturing the next generation of bakers through comprehensive hands-on certificate courses and weekend kids' workshops.
